### Root cause: the learn-reliability studies (`docs/evaluation/results/2026-10-22-corpus-v2/`) metered nothing

- **What ran.** Five studies, 17:06–18:35 UTC, through a test harness. They wrote logs (`eval-runs/learn-reliability*.log`) but no `UsageEvent`s.
- **The gap.** In 16:00–18:59 UTC the bill has 12.45 M prompt tokens and 1.15 M output tokens, of which 0.85 M and 0.07 M are metered. That is about half of the day's model tokens: ≈ $9.53 of model cost by token share, against $0.62 metered in the window.
- **Independent evidence.** The studies' own logs record their spend: $1.12 + $2.14 + $2.11 + $1.00 + $0.51 = **$6.88**, priced with the table of the day, model calls only. The remaining ≈ $2.6 of the window is the token-share estimate's imprecision (cached and uncached input are not split per hour) plus the learn-path checks those studies ran first.
- **Elsewhere metering tracks the bill:** 0.93 on prompt tokens, 0.92 on output tokens. At 20:00 UTC, the hour of the corpus runs plus the API uploads, it is **1.00 on both**.
